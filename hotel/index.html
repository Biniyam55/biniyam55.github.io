The Luma experience

Thoughtful service, at every turn

From a slow morning to an evening in the city, our team makes every part of your stay feel effortless.

Spa & Wellness

Relax with restorative spa treatments, a tranquil sauna, and a fully equipped fitness studio, with tailored wellness experiences available throughout your stay.

Personal Concierge

Our concierge team is available around the clock to arrange dining, local experiences, transport, and the thoughtful details that make your visit your own.

Private Airport Transfers

Arrive in comfort with a private pickup coordinated to your flight. We can also arrange return journeys and chauffeur-driven transport during your stay.

Signature Dining

Discover Ethiopian flavors and international favorites made with seasonal ingredients, alongside relaxed breakfasts, attentive in-room dining, and a considered selection of wines and cocktails.

24-Hour Guest Services

From a late check-in to an early departure, our team is here day and night to help with housekeeping, room requests, wake-up calls, and anything else you need.
